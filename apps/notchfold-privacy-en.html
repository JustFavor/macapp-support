Privacy policy

Effective November 20, 2026

This policy covers NotchFold for Mac distributed through the Mac App Store. NotchFold works without an account, processes dashboard content on your Mac, and includes no advertising or third-party usage analytics SDKs.

Music playback and album artwork

With your permission, NotchFold reads the track title, artist, album, artwork, duration and playback position from Apple Music or Spotify to display the current track and respond to playback controls. LRC lyrics you import are stored locally.

When the player does not supply an artwork URL, NotchFold sends the track title and artist name to Apple’s iTunes Search service. It uses the album and other track details locally to match the results. To display remote artwork, your Mac requests the image from its hosting server. The receiving service can see your IP address. These requests go directly to the respective services. Apple Privacy Policy

Files, notes and the app launcher

Dropping a file into the tray stores a copy, file index and preview on your Mac. After manual or automatic removal, the copy and removal history may remain on your Mac so you can undo the removal. When you use AirDrop or system sharing, macOS passes the selected files to the recipient or service you choose.

Notes, widget layouts, favorites and preferences are stored locally. The launcher reads application names and icons from enabled folders. When you add a source folder, NotchFold stores a read-only access bookmark so it can open the folder on subsequent launches.

Calendar and camera

With calendar permission, NotchFold reads event titles, times and calendar names for the selected date and displays them on your Mac. The mirror card displays a live camera preview. It does not record or upload video.

Your permissions and local data

You can revoke Automation, Calendars or Camera access in macOS System Settings → Privacy & Security. Remove a custom folder in launcher sources, delete a note in its editor, or remove imported lyrics in the music card’s lyrics menu.

To erase all local Mac App Store data, back up any files you want to keep, quit NotchFold, then use Finder to remove its sandbox container at ~/Library/Containers/com.XJ.NotchFold. This deletes staged copies, undo history and settings in the container.
