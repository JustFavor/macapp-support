NotchFold Support

NotchFold puts music, calendar events, a focus timer, a file tray and an app launcher at the top of your Mac screen. The Mac App Store edition supports macOS 14.5 or later on Apple silicon and Intel Macs.

Open the panel and settings

Click the panel at the top center of the screen. The three icons at the upper left switch between the dashboard, file tray and launcher. The gear opens settings. Use the home section to choose and reorder dashboard cards.

Connect Spotify or Apple Music

Start playing a track in the desktop player. Open NotchFold’s music settings, select the player and authorize the connection. If you previously declined, enable access for the player under System Settings → Privacy & Security → Automation.

Find a staged file

Open the file tray to search, preview or share a stored copy. You can undo a removal. The file tray settings control retention and what happens after you drag a file out.

Add an application folder

Open the app launcher settings and check that its source folder is enabled. If the app is installed elsewhere, add that folder and refresh the list.

Contact the developer

Email justfat.gh@gmail.com with your macOS and NotchFold versions, steps to reproduce the issue, and what you expected to happen. You can also post a public report on GitHub Issues.

Read the NotchFold Privacy Policy for details on artwork requests, permissions and local storage.
